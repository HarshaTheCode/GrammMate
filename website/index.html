Installation Guide

Load GrammMate directly into your browser in under a minute.

  1. 01
    Download Workspace Root

    Clone or download the project files into a folder on your computer.

  2. 02
    Open Extensions Page

    Navigate to chrome://extensions/ (or brave://extensions/, edge://extensions/).

  3. 03
    Enable Developer Mode

    Toggle the **Developer mode** switch in the top-right corner to **ON**.

  4. 04
    Load Unpacked Folder

    Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left and select the root folder of the project.

  1. 01
    Create OpenRouter Account

    Create an account and key at openrouter.ai/keys (completely free tiers are available).

  2. 02
    Click Extension Icon

    Click the GrammMate icon in your browser toolbar and click **Options**.

  3. 03
    Save Your Settings

    Enter your API key and preferred model name, then hit Save. GrammMate is ready to assist you!
